Woodbridge Starbucks is presented as a contemporary coffeehouse design concept that combines modern commercial architecture with biophilic design principles. The building emphasizes an expressive curved façade, vertical wooden elements, and expansive glass openings that establish a connection between the interior and its surrounding environment. The design seeks to provide a comfortable social destination while promoting natural lighting, efficient spatial organization, and customer accessibility.

Architectural Spaces

  • Main indoor seating and dining area
  • Coffee preparation and service counter
  • Beverage pickup station
  • Food preparation and storage area
  • Outdoor seating and landscaped spaces
  • Customer restrooms
  • Staff and utility facilities
  • Customer parking and pedestrian access
